- Stakeholders

## Purpose
Define a modular inspection and laboratory coordination service that retailers, warehouses, and third-party testers can adopt to validate suspect product batches quickly.

## Stakeholders
1. Retail operations teams.
2. Warehouse and distribution operators.
3. Accredited laboratories.
4. Corporate food safety and compliance teams.
5. Regulators and public health investigators.

## Functional Capabilities

### Specimen Registration
- Generate sample identifier.
- Capture chain of custody fields.
- Bind specimen to complaint and product identifiers.

### Inspection Request Routing
- Route to nearest capable laboratory based on test panel requirements.
- Support surge balancing for high-volume incidents.
- Track service level agreements for turnaround time.

### Test Panel Management
- Define contamination panel templates by product category.
- Support pathogen, toxin, and adulteration panel requests.
- Record methodology and detection thresholds.

### Results and Decision Engine
- Normalize laboratory outputs.
- Trigger hold, remove, or release decisions.
- Notify all affected stores and warehouses automatically.

### Audit and Reporting
- Maintain immutable chain of custody and action history.
- Generate regulator-ready inspection packets.
- Provide trend analytics across manufacturers and regions.

## Inspection Workflow
1. Complaint cluster reaches inspection threshold.
2. Batch hold instruction is issued.
3. Samples are collected with verified custody.
4. Laboratory performs assigned panel.
5. Results are reviewed against risk rules.
6. Product disposition decision is executed and tracked.

## Commercial Deployment Model
- Offer as a subscription module to retail chains and warehouse groups.
- Include integration kit for existing inventory and incident systems.
- Include compliance dashboard for corporate and regulator stakeholders.
- Include training package for on-site adoption and quality assurance.
