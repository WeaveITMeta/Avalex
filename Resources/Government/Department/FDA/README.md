## Purpose
This folder defines a conceptual platform that helps stores, labs, victims, and regulators act quickly on food safety complaints and recall actions.

The design goal is to reduce the time between complaint submission and shelf removal by creating a coordinated, evidence-driven workflow.

## Design Principles
1. Speed first for high-risk products.
2. Strong evidence capture for regulators and inspections.
3. Clear accountability for stores, distributors, and warehouse operators.
4. Traceability from customer report to final product disposition.
5. Safe quarantine handling to prevent resale of suspect returns.

## Expected Outcomes
- Faster product risk detection.
- Faster shelf action and quarantine action.
- Better coordination with Food and Drug Administration recall effectiveness goals.
- Better support for victims through a clear and convenient reporting experience.
