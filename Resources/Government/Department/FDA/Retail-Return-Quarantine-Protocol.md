# Retail Return Quarantine Protocol

## Table of Contents
- Purpose
- Scope
- Standard Operating Workflow
- Staff Training Requirements
- Compliance Verification

## Purpose
Prevent suspect returned food products from being placed back into sale inventory and ensure rapid quarantine and escalation.

## Scope
Applies to customer service counters, stock rooms, retail shelves, warehouse intake, and reverse logistics areas.

## Standard Operating Workflow

### Step 1: Intake at Customer Service
- Mark product as suspect immediately.
- Capture product photos and return reason.
- Capture product identifiers from label and container.
- Assign quarantine case identifier.

### Step 2: Physical Segregation
- Place product in tamper-evident quarantine container.
- Move container to restricted quarantine area.
- Do not place in resell, donation, or discount streams.

### Step 3: Digital Lock and Notification
- Change product status to quarantine in point-of-sale and inventory systems.
- Block all restock and transfer actions for matching product identifiers.
- Notify store manager, food safety lead, and corporate compliance immediately.

### Step 4: Recall and Risk Check
- Query recall and complaint intelligence services for matching signals.
- If high-risk signal exists, trigger shelf sweep and backroom sweep tasks.
- Verify all matching units are removed and quarantined.

### Step 5: Evidence and Disposition
- Record chain of custody.
- Store photos and task completion evidence.
- Route sample to laboratory inspection when required.
- Follow approved disposition path (destroy, hold, or regulator-approved rework).

## Staff Training Requirements
- Quarterly training for customer service, stock, and manager roles.
- Scenario drills for contaminated dairy and child nutrition products.
- Pass-fail competency checks for quarantine workflow.
- Incident debrief after every major recall action.

## Compliance Verification
- Daily quarantine area audit.
- Weekly random return transaction audit.
- Monthly shelf and backroom recall effectiveness checks.
- Escalation protocol for repeat non-compliance.
